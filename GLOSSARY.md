# Sergio Portfolio

The personal portfolio of Sergio Rodríguez Gundín: who he is, where he has worked and what he has built.

## Visual language

**Design system**:
The source of truth for the portfolio's look and voice (palette, type, spacing, components), kept in `docs/design-system/`.
_Avoid_: theme, style guide, UI kit

**Palette colour**:
One of the four design system colours (paper, night, tennis-ball, ultramarine) or their two tints.
_Avoid_: base colour, primary/secondary (as colour names)

**Third-party brand colour**:
A colour that belongs to someone else's identity: a client or employer (Celonis, Dixper, LaLiga, Bankinter, Dreamfit…) or a technology (Angular, React, AWS…). It appears only inside that party's own logo or icon and is not a palette colour.
_Avoid_: project brand colour, company colour, theme colour

**Theme**:
One of the two ways the palette is laid out: light (paper surface, night text) or dark (night surface, paper text).
_Avoid_: mode, skin
